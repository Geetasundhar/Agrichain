AgriChain

AgriChain is a blockchain-based smart agriculture supply chain platform that connects Farmers, Buyers, Retailers, and Admins in a secure and transparent system.
Farmers can register lands using geofencing and blockchain technology, add crops with QR codes, and manage farm data securely.
Buyers and retailers can purchase crops and verify product authenticity through QR-based traceability. 
The project uses React.js, Node.js, MongoDB, Solidity, Hardhat, and Ganache for full-stack blockchain integration.
AgriChain improves transparency, trust, and efficiency in agricultural management and supply chain operations.
